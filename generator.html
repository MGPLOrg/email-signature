Arth email signature generator

Fill in the details and choose a photo. Copy signature copies exactly what the preview shows, straight away.

To add a photo, upload it on GitHub to assets/photos (Add file → Upload files), wait a minute and reload this page. Use a square photo, or a round cut-out exported from Figma (106×106 or 212×212).

Preview

Steps

  1. Type the details and choose the photo.
  2. Click Copy signature.
  3. In Gmail open Settings → See all settings → Signature, paste it in place of the old one and click Save changes at the bottom.
